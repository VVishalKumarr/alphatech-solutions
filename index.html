About the contractor

From the incomer to the last socket.

A Vijayanagar electrical contracting firm working across residential and commercial properties since 2017.

The team handles the heavy end of the job, from erecting panels and terminating HT and LT cables to electrifying industrial sites, and the everyday end too: wiring, inspections, earthing and motor repairs. Liaisoning services are offered as part of the work.

The office is on the first floor of Balaji Complex on 15th Main, a short walk from Vijayanagar Metro Station. Contact person: Rajegowda.
